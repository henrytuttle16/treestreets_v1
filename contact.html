Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom order take?

Lead times vary by project complexity, but typically range from 2-6 weeks. I'll provide a specific timeline when we discuss your project.

Do you ship?

Yes! I ship within the continental US. Shipping costs will be calculated based on size and destination.

Can I choose the type of wood?

Absolutely! I work with various hardwoods including maple, walnut, cherry, oak, and exotic species. We'll discuss options for your project.

What's your return policy?

Since each piece is custom-made, I work closely with you throughout the process to ensure satisfaction. Contact me if you have concerns.
